# Try the PECA Impact Atlas

PECA lets you explore evidence about how decisions in one place can affect people elsewhere. [Open the live Atlas](https://weidufanling.au/peca-impact-atlas/).

## Try an exact result

- [New Zealand to Tonga: household income](https://weidufanling.au/uploads/2026/peca-atlas-v0.10.0-share-links.html#record=rse_household_income_top_ps2_fe&case=NZL&partner=TON&year=2007-2010). Open the seasonal work study estimate and check the units and standard error.
- [China to USA: PM₂.₅ and consumption](https://weidufanling.au/uploads/2026/peca-atlas-v0.10.0-share-links.html#record=pm25_mortality_2010_consumption&case=CHN&partner=USA&year=2010). Open the 2010 estimate of premature deaths in the US that the study's model attributes to supply chains serving consumption in China. Check what the reported interval covers.

Both links open the exact record with its source, units and reported uncertainty.

## Tell us what you find

[Open an issue](https://github.com/weidufanling/peca-feedback/issues/new) for a bug, confusing label, accessibility problem, source correction or idea for an add-on. You can also talk through an idea in **#the-workshop** in [Weidu Commons](https://discord.gg/vQN7M84crF).

A few lines are plenty. Tell us what you tried, what happened and what you'd change. For a source correction, include a public link and a page or table number if you have one. Please leave out private information.

This repository is for feedback. The playable Atlas and its evidence downloads are on the Weidu Fanling site.
